Privacy

Short version: Yaperino has no servers of its own, no accounts with us and no tracking. What you do in it stays on your computer.

The app

  • No telemetry. Yaperino doesn't collect usage data, crash reports or statistics, and doesn't send anything to its makers.
  • Your settings and sign-ins stay on your computer, in %APPDATA%\Yaperino on Windows, ~/Library/Application Support/Yaperino on macOS and ~/.local/share/yaperino on Linux. Delete that folder and everything is gone.
  • It talks to the services you use, directly: Twitch, Kick, YouTube (Google) and TikTok for chat, live status and sign-in; 7TV, BetterTTV and FrankerFaceZ for emotes. Their own privacy policies apply to what you send them. Your sign-ins are only ever sent to the platform they belong to.
  • When you aren't signed in to Twitch, the live status of channels is asked from api.ivr.fi, a public Twitch information service.
  • When a Twitch chat opens, its recent messages are loaded from recent-messages.robotty.de, the public chat history service Chatterino uses (it learns which channel you opened). You can turn this off in Settings.
  • Translation only happens when you pick Translate on a message or turn on "Auto-translate this chat". Then the message's text (nothing else) is sent to Google's translator, or to DeepL with your own key if you chose DeepL in Settings. Your DeepL key stays on your computer and is never put in settings backups.
  • Image upload only happens when you paste an image into a message box and confirm it. It goes to the host set in Settings (by default kappa.lol).
  • Plugins you install yourself can only use the network or files when you grant them that permission.

This website

  • No cookies, no analytics, no ads, and nothing loaded from other sites: no external fonts or scripts.
  • The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, and the downloads come from GitHub. Like any web server, GitHub receives your IP address and may log visits for security; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • The small script on the home page only checks which system you use, to offer the right download. It sends nothing anywhere.
